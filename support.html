Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Tiny Biomes is a fully offline, single-player game. You can play anytime, anywhere.

Where is my progress saved?
Your progress is saved locally on your device. Deleting the app will remove your saved data.

I lost my progress / found a bug. What can I do?
Please email us at the address above with a short description of what happened, and we'll do our best to help.
